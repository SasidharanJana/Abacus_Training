Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This app is built to work with as little data collection as possible. Here's exactly what happens with your information.

What we don't collect

We do not run a server, and we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to ourselves. There is no account system, no analytics tracking, and no advertising.

What's stored on your device

  • Your exam settings (digits, timing, level selection) — stored locally in your browser, never sent anywhere.
  • Your license/unlock status — stored locally on your device so you don't have to re-enter your key every time.

None of this leaves your device except when checking your license key's validity, described below.

Voice input

If you use the microphone/voice-answer feature, your speech is processed by your browser's built-in speech recognition service (typically provided by Google, on Chrome). We never receive, see, or store any audio or transcript — this happens entirely between your browser and that service.

Payments and license keys

Purchases are handled entirely by Lemon Squeezy, who act as the merchant of record for this app. We never see or store your payment card details. Lemon Squeezy issues a license key after purchase, which the app checks against Lemon Squeezy's license servers to confirm it's valid. See Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy for how they handle billing and customer information.

Children's privacy

This app is intended to be used by children under the guidance of a parent or instructor. No personal information is collected from any user, including children, to use the free (Foundation-level) content.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
